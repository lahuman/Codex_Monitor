# Editorial monitoring design

## Why
Account identities are incorrectly used as quota legends, and oversized duplicate chart cards waste monitoring space.

## What Changes
Use the supplied cream/coral editorial reference as desktop-scoped design tokens with accessible dark mode and native font fallbacks. Consolidate quota, observations, trend and settings under one account header. Legends contain only compact data-series labels. Preserve actual quota, history, alerts, privacy, login and account actions. Replace the generic multi-account donut with a small native SVG quota meter.
