# Menu bar typography hierarchy

## Why
The quota prefix looks like an unexpected symbol and identical text sizes make account names and percentages compete.

## What Changes
Remove the decorative quota prefix and render small regular account names above larger bold percentages, matching the user's reference. Preserve independent account clicks and meaningful unknown/stale/error/paused indicators. Keep native template rendering for Retina and appearance adaptation.

## Impact
macOS presentation and release identity only; no account data, polling or panel lifecycle changes.
