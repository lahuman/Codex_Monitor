# Unify monitor readability

## Why
Mobile cards are compact, but account management, desktop monitoring and portaled dialogs retain inconsistent tiny text, oversized quota tiles and narrow-screen overflow risks.

## What Changes
Use shared typography and quota color tokens, compact account-management bars from canonical windows, visible focus and accessible controls, responsive dialogs and clearer primary actions. Preserve monitoring semantics and every existing action.

## Impact
Frontend presentation across Android, desktop and the menu panel; no provider or persistence changes.
