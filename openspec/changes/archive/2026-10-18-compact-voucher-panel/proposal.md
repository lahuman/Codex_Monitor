# Compact voucher panel

## Why
The menu popup voucher card duplicates warnings and spends too much vertical space on dates and actions, pushing history below the viewport.

## What Changes
Retain exactly one nearest-expiry voucher row initially and an explicit full-list action. Condense popup heading, warning badge and action onto one line with a single compact expiry/countdown row, and reduce surrounding popup spacing without hiding history or error/unknown information. Keep dashboard detail presentation intact.
